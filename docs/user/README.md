# Dungerra Player Guide

Dungerra is an online top-down multiplayer shooter. You and other players explore an endless, dark world of walls and corridors. Guards patrol it, turrets watch over it, and shops are scattered through it. You carry a torch that lights a small circle around you. Everything outside it stays dark unless someone else's torch lights it, or you put on night-vision goggles.

Destroy enemies to earn money, spend it on better weapons, and try to stay alive. When you die, your score goes on the global leaderboard.

## Game at a glance

- **Multiplayer sessions.** Each session is its own persistent world. Its default limit is 10 players at once, and the host can set a different one. Sessions can be public or protected by a password.
- **Endless world.** New areas are generated as players explore, and the world is saved, so you can come back to it later.
- **Darkness.** You see enemies, items and bullets only inside a light source. Walls are always visible within your sight range.
- **Four weapons.** You start with an infinite-ammo blaster and can buy a shotgun, a rocket launcher and a railgun.
- **Four enemy types.** Patrolling soldiers, fast-firing lieutenants, snipers with laser sights, and heavy rocket towers.
- **Economy.** Kills earn money. Spend it in shops on weapons, ammo, aid kits and night-vision goggles.
- **PvP.** Other players can shoot you, and you can shoot them.
- **Leaderboard.** Your best run in each session is recorded when you die.

> Key bindings depend on the game client you use. This guide describes actions ("forward", "strafe left", "aim", "shoot", "use item"), not specific keys.

*Describes server game version 1.4.0.*
