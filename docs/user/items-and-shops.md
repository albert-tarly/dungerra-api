# Items, Loot and Shops

## Inventory

Your inventory holds:

| Item | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Blaster** | Starting weapon. Never lost. |
| **Shotgun**, **Rocket Launcher**, **Railgun** | Extra weapons. You can own one of each. |
| **Shotgun shells**, **Rockets**, **Railgun slugs** | Ammo for the matching weapon |
| **Aid kit** | Restores 1 life |
| **Night-vision goggles** | 20 seconds of night vision |
| **Money** | Spend it in shops |

### Using items

Each item has a hotkey in your client. The action happens when you **release** the key:

- **Weapon** – selects that weapon.
- **Aid kit** – restores **1 life**, up to the maximum of 6. *It is used up even if you are already at full health.*
- **Goggles** – turn on night vision for **20 seconds**. Using more while they are active adds another 20 seconds each. See [Light and vision](playing.md#night-vision-goggles).

## Picking things up

Walk over an item on the ground to pick it up. Its contents go straight into your inventory. Aid kits and goggles are **stored, not used**, so you decide when to use them.

There are three kinds of item on the ground:

| Item | Where it comes from | Contains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aid kit** | Dropped by soldiers and lieutenants | 1 aid kit |
| **Goggles** | Dropped by soldiers and lieutenants | 1 pair of goggles |
| **Chest** | Left by lieutenants, snipers, destroyed towers and dead players | A mix of items and/or money |

You only see items on the ground when they are in light (see [Light and vision](playing.md#light-and-vision)).

### Guard chests

- **Lieutenants** sometimes drop a chest with a **shotgun**. About 15% of lieutenants do.
- **Snipers** usually drop a chest. Each item is rolled separately: railgun slugs (50%, 5–20 slugs), a railgun (50%) and goggles (75%).

Finding a weapon you already own just adds another copy to your inventory.

### Tower chests

A destroyed tower always leaves a chest. Each type of content is rolled separately:

| Content | Chance | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shotgun shells | 50% | 5–20 |
| Rockets | 50% | 5–20 |
| Railgun slugs | 50% | 5–20 |
| Aid kits | 30% | 1–3 |
| Goggles | 20% | 1–2 |

Tower chests are the best free source of ammo in the game.

### Player chests

A player who dies drops a chest holding a random ⅓ to all of their money and of each item they carried, including weapons. Anyone can take it. It disappears after **5 minutes**. See [Dying](playing.md#dying).

## Money

You earn money by killing:

| Target | Money |
| --- | --- |
| Soldier | 20 |
| Lieutenant | 50 |
| Sniper | 100 |
| Tower | 200 |
| Another player | 100 |

You also gain money from the coins in other players' chests. You lose all your money when you die, so spend it.

## Shops

Every area of the world has one shop at its centre, and you usually spawn next to one. Each shop has its own name, such as *Bob's Armory* or *Shotgun Shack*, and its own random stock.

### How to buy

1. Walk into the shop. You need to be standing on the shop itself, within about 57 units of its centre. Your client will show you are inside.
2. Press the purchase hotkey for the item you want. The purchase happens when you release the key.

A purchase fails if:

- the shop doesn't sell that item or has sold out,
- you can't afford it,
- it is a weapon you already own.

### Prices

| Item | Price | You receive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shotgun | 500 | 1 shotgun |
| Rocket Launcher | 1,000 | 1 rocket launcher |
| Railgun | 1,500 | 1 railgun |
| Shotgun shells | 200 | pack of 10 |
| Rockets | 150 | pack of 5 |
| Railgun slugs | 300 | pack of 10 |
| Aid kit | 50 | 1 aid kit |
| Night-vision goggles | 100 | 1 pair |

### Stock

Every shop has **limited stock** that is set when its area is first created and **never restocks**. Every player in the session shares the same stock, so if someone buys the last railgun, it is gone.

| Item | Chance a shop sells it | Starting stock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each weapon | 70% | 2–5 |
| Each ammo type | 70% | 10–20 packs |
| Aid kits | 50% | 10–20 |
| Goggles | 30% | 3–6 |

If a shop doesn't have what you need, try the shop in a neighbouring area.
