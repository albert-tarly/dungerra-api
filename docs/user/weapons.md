# Weapons

You always have the **blaster**. You can buy the other three weapons in [shops](items-and-shops.md#shops) or take them from chests: lieutenants sometimes drop a shotgun, snipers often drop a railgun, and dead players drop whatever they carried.

To **switch weapons**, use the weapon's item hotkey in your client. You can only switch to a weapon you own. The blaster is always available.

| **Shop price** | Free (starting weapon) | 500 | 1,000 | 1,500 |

## Blaster

Your starting weapon, and the only one that never runs out.

- Holds **6 shots** and regains **1 shot per second** automatically, but only while the blaster is the weapon you have selected.
- Fires a visible bullet that travels across the map, so you have to lead moving targets.
- A bullet stops at the first wall or target it hits.

## Shotgun

A close-range weapon that hits several targets at once.

- Each shot fires **8 pellets** spread across a **30° cone**.
- Pellets hit instantly, reach **200 units**, and stop at walls.
- The total damage of 2 lives is split evenly between the pellets. Up close most pellets hit one target and do close to full damage. Further away the pellets spread out and fewer of them connect.
- Pellets **pass through** enemies and players, so lined-up targets all take damage.
- **Magazine:** holds 2 shells. While the shotgun is selected it reloads **1 shell every 2 seconds** from the shotgun shells in your inventory. When you run out of shells it stops reloading.

## Rocket Launcher

Heavy area damage.

- Fires a slow rocket that **explodes** when it hits a wall, an enemy or a player.
- A direct hit deals **2 damage** to the target.
- The explosion damages everything else within **150 units**, up to 2 damage at the centre, dropping to nothing at the edge.
- **Watch out:** the explosion hurts you too, and it also damages other players. Don't fire at walls right next to you.
- Each shot uses one **rocket** from your inventory. There is no magazine.
- A rocket that hits nothing within 5 seconds fizzles out without exploding.

## Railgun

A long-range precision weapon.

- Fires an instant beam that reaches up to **1,500 units**, stopping at the first wall.
- Deals **3 damage** to *every* enemy and player along the beam.
- A soldier dies from one hit, and so do lieutenants and snipers (2 lives each). It is also the fastest way to wear down a tower.
- Each shot uses one **railgun slug** from your inventory. There is no magazine.

## Getting ammo

| Ammo | Used by | Shop pack | Pack price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shotgun shells | Shotgun | 10 | 200 |
| Rockets | Rocket launcher | 5 | 150 |
| Railgun slugs | Railgun | 10 | 300 |

You can also find ammo in the chests that destroyed **towers** leave behind, in **sniper** chests (railgun slugs), and in chests dropped by players who died. See [Items, Loot and Shops](items-and-shops.md).
