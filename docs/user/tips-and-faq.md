# Tips and FAQ

## Tips

**Survival**
- Use your 3 seconds of spawn invulnerability to get your bearings, not to charge a tower.
- Hurt? Walk over an aid kit: it heals you as you pick it up.
- Keep at least one aid kit, and don't use it at full health. It is used up and heals nothing.
- A red laser on you means a sniper is about to fire. You have about 2.5 seconds to get behind a wall.
- Watch your rocket splash. Firing at a wall right in front of you hurts you.

**Fighting**
- Guards only notice you once they are inside your torch light. The blaster's bullets fly further than that, so you can often shoot first from the edge of the light.
- Lieutenants keep moving while they shoot. Aim slightly ahead of them.
- Tower turrets turn slowly. Circle a tower and it will struggle to aim at you.
- The railgun and shotgun pass through targets. Line enemies up to hit several at once.
- Enemies block each other's line of sight. A guard standing behind another guard often can't shoot you.

**Economy**
- Destroy towers. They are worth 200, and their chests are full of ammo.
- Hunt snipers. Half of them drop a free railgun, and many drop railgun slugs.
- The shotgun (500) is the cheapest upgrade and very strong up close. Save for the railgun for long-range fights and towers.
- Shop stock is limited and shared. If you see the weapon you want, buy it before someone else does.
- You lose all your money when you die, so spend it before taking big risks.

**Stealth and teamwork**
- Night-vision goggles make you invisible to other players and let you see far into the dark. Use them to scout, to ambush, or to get away.
- Exploring with teammates lights up more of the map, because you see whatever their torches light.
- If you die, go back for your chest. You can see it even in the dark, and it lasts 5 minutes.

## FAQ

**Why can't I see the enemy that's shooting me?**
It is outside every light source. Move closer, put on goggles, or stay near teammates whose torches light the area.

**Why didn't my aid kit or goggles get used when I picked them up?**
Aid kits are only used on pickup while you are below 6 lives; at full health they go into your inventory. Goggles always go into your inventory. Use stored items with their hotkey.

**Why can't I switch to the shotgun / rocket launcher / railgun?**
You don't own it yet. Buy it in a shop or take it from a chest.

**My shotgun stopped reloading.**
You are out of shotgun shells. The magazine only reloads from shells in your inventory.

**My blaster isn't reloading.**
The blaster only recharges while it is your selected weapon.

**Why can't I buy something in the shop?**
You need to be standing inside the shop, have enough money, and the item must be in stock. You also can't buy a weapon you already own.

**Do enemies come back?**
No. Killed enemies are gone for good in that session.

**I closed the game. Did I lose my progress?**
No. Rejoin the same session and you continue where you left off. If you were dead, you respawn.

**Why can't I join a session?**
It is either full (counting only players who are online right now) or private and needs the correct password.

**When does my score show up on the leaderboard?**
When you die. The leaderboard keeps your best run in each session.

**Can other players hurt me?**
Yes. Every weapon damages other players, and killing a player earns 100 money and score.
