# Getting Started

## Signing in

Depending on how the server you play on is set up, you can sign in with **Google**, with a **local account** (a username and password you choose), or with either.

- **Google:** the first time you sign in, an account is created for you automatically. Your in-game name is the part of your email address before the `@` (for example, `jane.doe@gmail.com` plays as **jane.doe**).
- **Local account:** choose **Create account**, then pick a username (3–24 letters, digits, `_`, `-` or `.`) and a password of at least 8 characters. Other players see your name with `@local` added (for example, **jane@local**), so it can't be confused with a Google player of the same name. Usernames are not case-sensitive: `Jane` and `jane` are the same account.

Google and local accounts are separate. Signing in with Google and with a local account of the same name gives you two different characters, each with its own progress and leaderboard entries.

Other players see your name in game, and it appears on the leaderboard.

A sign-in lasts about 8 days. After that you are asked to sign in again.

## Game sessions

A **session** is a single shared game world. Everything that happens in it is kept for the next time anyone plays there: the map, which enemies are still alive, shop stock, and each player's progress.

### Browsing sessions

The session list shows the active sessions you can join, with each one's name, host and player limit.

### Creating a session

When you create a session you choose:

| Setting | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | 1–50 characters |
| **Max players** | How many players can be connected at the same time. Defaults to 10. |
| **Private** | If enabled, players need a password to join |
| **Password** | Required for private sessions |

You become the session's **host**. Only the host can delete a session.

### Joining a session

Pick a session from the list and join it. You can't join if:

- the session already has as many *connected* players as its limit (players who are offline don't count), or
- the session is private and you entered the wrong password.

## Your first spawn

When you enter a session for the first time:

- you appear near the centre of one of the world's areas, usually close to a **shop**,
- you have **6 lives**, a **blaster** with a full 6-shot magazine, and no money,
- you are **invulnerable for 3 seconds**, so you have time to look around.

## Leaving and coming back

You leave a session by closing the game or disconnecting. Your character is then put "on hold":

- other players and enemies can't see or hit it,
- your position, health, money, inventory and score are kept.

When you reconnect to the same session, you continue from the same spot with the same gear. If you were dead when you left, you respawn automatically when you return.

The world is saved every few minutes while people are playing, and again when the last player leaves.
