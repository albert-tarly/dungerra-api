# Enemies

The world is guarded by four kinds of enemies. Guards (soldiers, lieutenants and snipers) are posted next to walls. Every area also has one tower.

## Overview

| Enemy | Lives | Reward | Weapon | Fires every | Moves? | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Soldier** | 1 | 20 | Bullets (1 dmg) | 1.0 s | Patrols, stops to shoot | 30% chance: aid kit or goggles |
| **Lieutenant** | 2 | 50 | Bullets (1 dmg) | 0.5 s | Patrols, **keeps moving while shooting** | 50% chance: a shotgun, an aid kit or goggles |
| **Sniper** | 2 | 100 | Instant shot (1 dmg) | 2.5 s | Stationary | Usually: a chest with railgun gear and goggles |
| **Tower** | 30 | 200 | Rockets (2 dmg + splash) | 2.0 s | Stationary, turret turns | Always: a chest of supplies |

When a soldier drops an item, it is an aid kit 80% of the time and night-vision goggles 20% of the time. Lieutenant and sniper drops are described in their own sections below.

About 81% of guards are soldiers, 14% are lieutenants and 5% are snipers.

## How enemies notice you

Enemies wake up when any player comes within 1,500 units. A guard then has to *see* you before it attacks:

- **Soldiers, lieutenants and towers** notice you when they are inside your **torch light** (about 200 units from you). With night-vision goggles on, your torch is out and they only notice you within about **100 units**.
- **Snipers** can spot you from up to **1,500 units**, light or no light.
- Every enemy needs a **clear line of sight**. Walls block it, and so do other enemies standing in the way.

If several players are visible, an enemy targets the closest one.

## Soldier

The most common guard.

- Walks back and forth along its wall at 120 units/second.
- When it sees you, it **stops, turns to face you and fires** every second.
- Its bullets travel at 240 units/second, slowly enough to dodge at range.
- One hit from any weapon kills it.

## Lieutenant

A tougher, more aggressive soldier.

- Takes 2 hits from the blaster.
- Fires **twice as often** as a soldier.
- **Keeps patrolling while shooting**, so it is a harder target.
- Drops something half the time. Of those drops, 30% are a chest with a **shotgun**. The rest are an aid kit (80%) or goggles (20%).

## Sniper

A rare, dangerous guard with a laser sight.

- Never moves from its post.
- Spots you from up to 1,500 units away if nothing blocks the line of sight.
- When it picks a target it shows a **red laser sight** on that player. Anyone within range can see the laser, even in the dark.
- It fires **2.5 seconds after locking on**, and every 2.5 seconds after that. The shot hits **instantly**, so you can't dodge it once it has been fired.
- **Break line of sight** to make it lose you. Duck behind a wall before the 2.5 seconds are up. When it finds a new target, the 2.5-second countdown starts again.
- When killed, it usually leaves a chest. Each item is rolled separately: **railgun slugs** (50%, 5–20 slugs), a **railgun** (50%) and **goggles** (75%). If none of the rolls succeed, it drops nothing.

## Tower

A heavy turret. There is one in every area.

- Large (120 units across) and armoured: **30 lives**.
- Its turret **turns at 90° per second** and fires only when it is pointing straight at its target. Circling it quickly makes it struggle to keep up.
- Fires **rockets** every 2 seconds. Rockets explode on impact and do splash damage, like the player rocket launcher.
- Its rockets also hurt other enemies caught in the blast.
- When destroyed, its wreck stays on the map for 30 seconds, and it **always leaves a supply chest**. See [Items, Loot and Shops](items-and-shops.md#tower-chests).

## Friendly fire between enemies

Enemy bullets and rockets can hurt other enemies. You can sometimes get a lieutenant or a tower to damage a nearby guard, but you get no reward for kills you didn't make yourself.

## After death

Destroyed enemies leave a body on the map for a few seconds, 30 seconds for a tower, and then disappear. **Enemies never respawn**, so once an area is cleared it stays safe, apart from other players.
