# Score and Leaderboard

## Score

Your **score** is the total reward you have earned from kills during your current life:

| Kill | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Soldier | +20 |
| Lieutenant | +50 |
| Sniper | +100 |
| Tower | +200 |
| Another player | +100 |

These are the Hard values. The session's [difficulty](getting-started.md#difficulty) multiplies them, rounded to a whole number: ×0.25 on Peaceful, ×0.45 on Easy, ×1.2 on Nightmare, and between ×0.25 and ×2 on Custom, depending on how dangerous its settings are. A tower on Easy is worth 90 score, for example.

Score and money go up together when you make a kill, but they are separate:

- **Spending money doesn't lower your score.**
- The difficulty's money rate doesn't change your score. Double money on Easy doesn't mean double score.
- Money you take from other players' chests **doesn't add to your score**.

Each kill also adds 1 to your **kill count**.

Score, money and kills are all **reset to zero when you respawn**. Each life is a new run.

## Leaderboard

Your run is sent to the **global leaderboard when you die**. The leaderboard keeps **one entry per player per session**, and for that entry it records:

- your **best score** in a single life in that session,
- your **best kill count** in a single life in that session,
- how many times you have **died** in that session.

If your new run doesn't beat your previous best in that session, your best score stays as it was. The death count still goes up.

The global leaderboard ranks entries by score, highest first, and shows the top 100 by default. Each entry shows the player name, the score, the session it was set in, and when the entry was last updated.

> **Note:** a run counts only when it ends in death. If you leave the session while alive, your character and score wait for you to come back, but nothing is sent to the leaderboard yet.
